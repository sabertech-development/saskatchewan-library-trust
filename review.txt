BOUNDARIES
Private equity and privately owned business proceeds only. No borrowing, new government funding, donations, crowdfunding, sponsorship revenue or library user charges. Library assets, services and patron information are not transferred or pledged. No returns, capital preservation or payments are guaranteed. Existing library revenues only establish funding gaps.

MODEL LIMITS
Business cash is after business-level operating costs, taxes, maintenance capital expenditure and working capital. Vehicle costs are additional. Tax is a placeholder. New subscriptions, unrealized gains, exits and capital returns are excluded. Investor cash yield is not total return or IRR.

PROPOSED RESPONSIBILITIES

Governing board / trustees
Accountability for the proposed vehicle and its governing agreements.
Set reserve policy; approve investor distribution terms under governing agreements; authorize payments through dual sign-off; establish library eligibility after consultation; approve changes only through the agreed consent process.
Review conflicts and related-party transactions through independent members, with recusals, minutes and disclosure. No protection is legally established until governing documents provide it.

Investment manager
Steward the commercial portfolio and maintain cash forecasts.
Source businesses, perform due diligence, oversee management, recommend reinvestment and prepare cash-supported payment schedules.
Publish fees, related interests, performance and valuations. Reporting failures trigger escalation to the board and suspension of discretionary approvals.

Investment committee
Provide independent scrutiny of proposed acquisitions and portfolio risk.
Approve acquisitions within a documented board mandate; review concentration, operating risks and independent valuation evidence.
No conflicted member votes. Material transactions or mandate exceptions return to the board.

Library allocation committee
Apply the published eligibility and allocation rules independently.
Approve library awards within the available pool and delegated mandate; document scoring; provide reasons; refer appeals to an independent review panel.
Recuse members with applicant interests. Publish criteria and allocation results, subject to appropriate confidentiality.

Participating library boards
Retain authority over library assets, services and local priorities.
Submit substantiated funding gaps, accept funding agreements and review recipient use of funds and service outcomes.
Report use of funds and variances. Reporting failures trigger review, remediation and potentially future-payment suspension under agreed terms.

Independent auditor
Provide independent verification of financial reporting within an agreed audit scope.
Audit vehicle accounts and examine cash-source and use reconciliation; report findings to the governing board.
Independent appointment, access to records and disclosed findings. An audit does not guarantee future performance.

Legal and tax advisers
Assess structure, documentation, tax and regulatory obligations.
Review investor rights, offering requirements, legal enforceability, library agreements and the tax model before commitments.
Written professional assessment is not yet obtained. Preferred returns, carried interest and redemptions require revised modelling if introduced.

READINESS GATES

Provincial needs assessment
Prerequisites: Agreement on consultation scope and respectful engagement with potential participants.
Outputs: Funding-gap dataset; service priorities; potential participant consultation. No synthetic budget may substitute for verified need.
Owner: Proposed library working group and proposal sponsor
Gate: Is there a documented provincial requirement and a credible basis for eligibility?

Structure and commercial terms
Prerequisites: A documented needs assessment and initial investor appetite discussions.
Outputs: Structure options; investor rights; proposed distribution agreements; governance design; reserve and liquidity policies.
Owner: Legal and tax advisers, proposed governing board
Gate: Can proposed terms meet legal requirements and be acceptable to both investors and participating libraries?

Feasibility and independent assessment
Prerequisites: Defined structure options and testable commercial assumptions.
Outputs: Acquisition economics; investor appetite assessment; financial stress testing; legal and tax review.
Owner: Proposed investment committee and independent professional advisers
Gate: Does independently reviewed evidence justify proceeding, revising or stopping?

Capitalization and acquisitions
Prerequisites: Feasibility gate passed; finalized documents; all required approvals and disclosures.
Outputs: Executed agreements; subscribed private equity; completed due diligence; acquired businesses; funded reserve.
Owner: Proposed governing board and investment manager
Gate: Are capital, controls, businesses and the required reserve actually in place?

Library distributions and monitoring
Prerequisites: Positive available cash, approved eligibility and executed recipient agreements.
Outputs: Cash-supported awards; service reporting; audited accounts; review of allocation effectiveness and payment timing.
Owner: Proposed allocation committee, library boards and independent auditor
Gate: Do verified cash and recipient reporting support each payment and continued operation?
